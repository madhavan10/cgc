New India Guitar Festival 2026

New Delhi , India
March 19 , 2026 - March 22 , 2026
About
A 4-day weekend of classical guitar masterclasses and concerts - a ‘festival’ designed to attract classical guitarists - students, amateurs and aspiring musicians from Delhi and outside Delhi to attend concerts by classical guitarists from India, South Korea and Europe who have studied, taught and performed widely, inspiring young classical musicians and provide a platform to explore opportunities that lie in store for them in the future.
Schedule
Thursday, 19th March: - Daytime; Masterclasses at Delhi School of Music - Evening; Samrat Majumder, Ji Hyung Park feat. Madhavan Somanathan in concert at the Little Theatre Group Auditorium Near Mandi House Friday, 20th March: - Daytime; Masterclasses at Delhi School of Music - Evening; Madhavan Somanathan opening for Alexander Ramirez, guitar, and Sheila Arnold, piano, in concert at Delhi School of Music. Saturday 21st March: - Eid-al-fitr - Morning; Extra-curricular activity (visit to India Gate) - Afternoon; Public masterclasses at Madhavan Sir's house (F-7/10 Vasant Vihar, Basement Right) - Evening; Concert of the Participants Part 1 (Under-15) at Madhavan Sir's house. Sunday, 22nd March: - Daytime; Masterclasses at Delhi School of Music - Evening; Youth Mentors' concert at Delhi School of Music
Guests
Alexander Ramirez (guitar) Sheila Arnold (piano) Samrat Majumder (guitar) Madhavan Somanathan (guitar) Ji Hyung Park (guitar)

Practical Info
  • Event Fees
    active participants: Rs. 4,500/- passive participants: Rs. 3,500/- Passive package includes tickets to concert @ Little Theatre Group Auditorium on the 19th of March, reserved seats in front at DSM concerts, access to all public masterclasses and lectures/workshops. Parents and/or guardians are not recommended to buy the passive package for themselves but to buy tickets to any paid concerts separately, unless of course they wish to be part of the educational experience. Active package includes passive package and one masterclass
  • Venue Addresses
    Delhi School of Music @ 8, Nyaya Marg, Chanakya Puri - 110021, New Delhi Little Theatre Group Auditorium @ Copernicus Marg, opposite Doordarshan Bhawan, Mandi House - 110001, New Delhi

  • Travel Info
    Airports: Indira Gandhi International Airport (Terminals 1-3) Train stations: New Delhi, Old Delhi, Hazrat Nizamuddin
  • Other Facilities
